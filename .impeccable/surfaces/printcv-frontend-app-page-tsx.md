# Surface: PrintCV site (home leads; loja, produto, galeria, orçamento, sobre share the world)

Mode: Persuade (home), the other routes inherit the world. Audience: private gift buyers, local businesses, makers with an STL, tourists. Action: send one WhatsApp message (order or quote). Proof: real workshop photos, 4 timelapses with dates. Constraints: no invented claims (see PRODUCT.md), Portuguese (PT/CV), phone-first, two products without photo.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a pano di terra: every section is a woven band sewn to the next, the way a print is laid down layer on layer. Refuses the category default of a dark/glow "maker tech" hero or a white shop grid of floating cards.

OWN-WORLD: Two inks, no greys: deep indigo #141C44 and undyed cotton #F3F2EC, indigo #22327A for pattern ink; one black band only (the timelapse climax); cotton-yellow #F2C230 reserved exclusively for actions that open WhatsApp. Bands joined by visible stitched seams and narrow panu-obra pattern strips (lozenge, tooth, check) authored as SVG. Archivo variable: expanded heavy for display, normal width for text, condensed caps for data (material, date, price). No cards with shadows, no rounded pills, square corners, hierarchy by band width and field colour.

STORY: The visitor sees the printer working and a cloth growing in step with it, understands it is made here in Praia, believes it from real dates and photos, and sends a WhatsApp message describing the piece.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Indigo band, full width. Left: H1 in expanded heavy type (clamp 2.6rem→5.25rem at 5.2vw: ~4.7rem at 1440 in five lines, ~5.25rem at 1920; the 84rem frame and a 1.3fr title column cap it below 5.5rem) "Imprimimos em 3D, camada a camada, na Praia."; one line of support; a working quote field ("O que pretende imprimir?") with the yellow "Pedir pelo WhatsApp" button beside it; secondary text link to the loja. Right: the loom — the multicolour timelapse in a cotton frame with, beneath it at full width, an SVG cloth band (12 rows) whose rows fill bottom-up in sync with the video's progress. Mobile: H1, field+button, then the loom at full width.

FORM: Pano di Terra, my grounded list position 3 (assigned by the roll); raises from declined challengers: one warm colour reserved for action (nixie), two inks no greys (op-art), hierarchy by band width/field colour (glazier), a single black band for the climax (manga). seed:24146e580020cb60. Signature interaction: the loom synced to the timelapse. Motion grammar: stepped, bottom-up row reveals (layers), nothing floats or glows.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
